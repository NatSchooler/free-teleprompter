How to use this teleprompter ↓

A free online teleprompter for video, webinars and live talks

This free teleprompter runs in your web browser. Paste your script, choose a comfortable speed and read straight to camera, with no download, no account and no watermark. It is built for YouTube videos, social clips, podcasts, online courses, webinars, Zoom and Teams calls, presentations and live streams.

The whole tool is a single HTML file. Your script never leaves your device, and once the page has loaded it keeps working offline.

How to use the teleprompter

  1. Click Edit Script, then type or paste your script. You can also open a .txt file.
  2. Click Save & Open Prompter.
  3. Set the Size and Speed sliders, and turn on the Countdown if you want three seconds to get ready.
  4. Press Space or click Start. Keep your eyes on the line beside the arrow marker.
  5. Press ↑ or ↓ to speed up or slow down while you read, and Space to pause.

Features

  • Smooth auto-scroll with speed you can change mid-read
  • Mirror mode for teleprompter glass and beam-splitter rigs, plus an upside-down flip
  • Webcam overlay so you can check your framing behind the text
  • Countdown, full screen and a progress bar
  • Keyboard shortcuts for hands-free control
  • Automatic saving of your script and settings in your browser
  • Private and offline: no tracking, no cookies, no uploads

Keyboard shortcuts

Space start or pause · ↑ ↓ speed · ← → nudge the text · + − text size · Home back to the top · M mirror · V flip · C camera · F full screen · E editor · Esc leave the editor

Tips for reading a teleprompter naturally

  • Place the browser window as close to your camera lens as you can, so your eyes stay near the lens.
  • Start slower than you think you need, then raise the speed as you settle in.
  • Write short lines and leave a blank line between paragraphs, so you have natural places to breathe.
  • Read it through once out loud before you record, and mark any words that trip you up.

Frequently asked questions

Is this teleprompter really free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no account, no trial and no watermark. The code is open source under the MIT licence.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No. It runs in your web browser. You can also save the page as a single HTML file and use it offline.

Can I use it with teleprompter glass?

Yes. Turn on Mirror to reverse the text for beam-splitter glass, and Flip if your rig needs the text upside down.

Is my script uploaded anywhere?

No. Your script and settings stay in your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server and there is no tracking.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

Yes. It works in modern browsers on iPad, Android tablets, phones, Windows, macOS, Linux and Chromebook.
